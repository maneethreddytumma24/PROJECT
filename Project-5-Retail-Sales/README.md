# 🛒 Project 5: Retail Sales Analysis Dashboard

## 📌 Objective

The objective of this project is to analyze retail sales data and generate meaningful business insights using Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA). The project focuses on data cleaning, statistical analysis, filtering, grouping, visualization, and business recommendations.

---

## 📂 Dataset

- **Dataset:** Sample Superstore Dataset
- **Source:** Kaggle
- **Records:** 9,994
- **Features:** 21

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Libraries

- Python
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Matplotlib
- Google Colab

---

## 📋 Tasks Performed

- Imported and explored the dataset
- Checked missing values
- Removed duplicate records
- Calculated descriptive statistics
- Detected and treated outliers using the IQR method
- Performed correlation analysis
- Filtered and sorted the dataset
- Performed GroupBy analysis
- Created business visualizations
- Generated an executive summary

---

## 📊 Visualizations

- 📊 Bar Graph – Sales by Category
- 📈 Line Graph – Sales Trend
- 📉 Histogram – Sales Distribution
- 🔵 Scatter Plot – Sales vs Profit
- 🌡️ Correlation Heatmap

---

## 📈 Business Insights

- Technology generated the highest sales revenue.
- Office Supplies had the largest number of orders.
- Furniture products showed inconsistent profitability.
- Some high-sales orders resulted in losses due to heavy discounts.
- The West and East regions contributed significantly to total sales and profit.

---

## 💡 Recommendations

- Reduce excessive discounts on low-profit products.
- Increase focus on high-performing product categories.
- Improve marketing in underperforming regions.
- Monitor products with high sales but low profitability.
- Optimize pricing strategies to improve profit margins.
